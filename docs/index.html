WooCommerce build · portfolio project

Lereng Coffee Roasters

A complete WooCommerce store for a (fictional) specialty coffee roaster in Bali: local payments, delivery by province, subscriptions, wholesale, gift cards and a bilingual storefront, with a year of order history.

Open the live store Source on GitHub

Runs real WordPress + WooCommerce in your browser via WordPress Playground. First load ≈ 1 minute (it downloads WordPress, WooCommerce and the store).
Admin: admin / password · payments are a sandbox · emails go to the Demo inbox.

Things to try

  1. Filter the coffee by process or taste, pick a size and grind, add to cart.
  2. Check out with QRIS or a virtual account, then press “Simulate a successful payment” on the payment page.
  3. Switch the header to ID: the whole store, including checkout and emails, in Bahasa Indonesia.
  4. Choose “Subscribe & save” on a coffee; manage it under My account → Subscriptions.
  5. Build a Discovery Box, buy a gift card, track an order, apply for wholesale.
  6. Sign in to the admin (/wp-admin): Roastery today, Roast list, Subscriptions MRR, Analytics with a year of sales.

What's built

Catalogue

66 products, 38 coffees with 550+ size × grind variations; filters, coffee facts, brew recipes, farm stories, pre-order, micro-lots.

Delivery

JNE, SiCepat and GoSend rates by province and weight, free-delivery thresholds, roastery pickup, cold brew limited to south Bali.

Payments

Sandbox QRIS and BCA/Mandiri/BNI/BRI virtual accounts modelled on Midtrans: signed webhooks, expiry, refunds.

Subscriptions

No plugin: renewals before roast day, payment links, skip, pause, change, cancel, MRR.

Fulfilment

Roast days with a 10:00 cut-off, roast list, bulk roasting, waybills, tracking page, review emails, PPN invoices.

Promotions

First-order and minimum-spend coupons, gift cards with a ledger, Discovery Box and bundles with component stock.

Wholesale

Applications, approval, tiered prices, 5 kg minimum, quick order, invoice payment.

Bilingual

English / Bahasa Indonesia per visitor, content included; orders remember the language for later emails.

Lereng is a fictional brand. Origins and processes are real; producers and the roastery are invented. Photos: CC0, Wikimedia Commons.
